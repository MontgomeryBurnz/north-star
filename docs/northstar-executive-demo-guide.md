# NorthStar Executive Product Summary and Demo Guide

Last updated: October 22, 2026

## Executive Summary

NorthStar is an intelligence hub for complex program delivery. It helps delivery teams, practice leaders, executives, and clients separate signal from noise by combining program setup, uploaded artifacts, team updates, leadership feedback, delivery board activity, curated client-facing updates, and governance decisions into living guidance and role-specific work products.

NorthStar's product promise:

> Where people, intelligence, and data move as one.

The application is designed for consulting and enterprise delivery environments where leaders need fast visibility into program health, teams need practical next actions, and clients need a clean executive view of progress, risks, and decisions.

## Why NorthStar Exists

Complex programs create too much fragmented signal:

- Requirements and scope live in artifacts.
- Status lives in meetings, updates, and team conversations.
- Risks and decisions live across scattered notes and leadership feedback.
- Role-specific work products are often rebuilt manually.
- Executives and clients need clarity without reading every detail.

NorthStar connects these inputs and turns them into structured action:

- What is happening?
- What changed?
- What needs attention?
- What should each role do next?
- What should the team create to move the work forward?
- What evidence supports the recommendation?
- Who needs to approve, dispute, or govern the output?

## Core Value Proposition

NorthStar gives leaders and teams one operating system for program intelligence:

- Program Hub: set up new programs, manage active programs, track progress, and operate the delivery board.
- Guided Plans: translate live program signal into program-level guidance and role-based team action plans.
- Studio: turn discovery sources into a saved full product package, refine with feedback, review stories with explicitly shared collaborators, and download editable Office files. AI readiness and human approval remain separate.
- Leadership: collect concise leadership feedback and automatically refresh guidance.
- Client Portal: provide clients with an executive view of portfolio and program progress.
- Admin: manage users, roles, governance, audit history, cost posture, model settings, and platform health.

## Executive Application Review

NorthStar is designed to improve how consulting and enterprise teams deliver complex work across cross-functional roles, client portfolios, and leadership groups.

### Delivery Improvement

NorthStar gives teams a single operating system for program execution. Program setup, role footprint, weekly role updates, delivery board movement, artifacts, leadership feedback, and client-facing updates are connected so teams do not have to rebuild the program story manually every week.

### Cross-Functional Alignment

Each role has a clear place to contribute signal and receive role-centric guidance. Product Management, Business Analysis, User Experience, Application Development, Data Engineering, Change Management, Scrum Master, Delivery Lead, and other custom roles can each see what matters most to their lane while still preserving program-level visibility.

### Centralized Program Intelligence

NorthStar centralizes the work context that usually lives across meetings, documents, status notes, decision logs, and informal updates. The result is a shared intelligence layer that keeps guidance, artifacts, leadership views, and client updates grounded in the same program record.

### Human-in-the-Loop Guidance

NorthStar uses AI-guided synthesis to help interpret program signal, but people remain in control. Users provide the source inputs, leaders provide direction, Admins govern access and quality, and client-facing updates remain intentionally curated through dedicated client update fields.

### Leadership and Portfolio Visibility

Leadership users can monitor posture, risk, decisions, and progress across programs without needing to read every tactical update. The Client Portal gives executive sponsors a cleaner portfolio and program view, while Admin provides auditability, cost posture, model settings, and platform health.

### Client Differentiator

For consulting delivery, NorthStar can become a differentiator because it gives clients a polished executive view while protecting internal working context. It helps consulting teams show disciplined delivery, structured insight, clear accountability, and evidence-backed progress.

## Primary User Groups

### Explorers

Explorers evaluate NorthStar using connected fictional data and prepared Studio outputs. They can navigate the product, inspect summaries, gaps, stories, criteria, tests, and Delivery handoff mapping previews, and download sample Office files. They cannot upload, change data, approve, share, adjust settings, invoke AI or public research, or send work externally. Sample mode is mandatory and no program assignment is required. For a no-paid-generation demo, use the prepared outputs only. Normal hosting/authentication/download traffic still uses infrastructure resources.

### Delivery Leads

Delivery Leads use NorthStar to manage program execution, track role-level signals, maintain the delivery board, and keep program guidance current.

### Team Members

Team members use NorthStar to provide focused updates for their assigned role, manage delivery actions, attach supporting artifacts, and generate role-specific outputs.

### Leadership

Leaders use NorthStar to review concise program posture, progress, risk, and decision needs, then provide guidance that refreshes the plan.

### Clients

Clients use NorthStar to see a curated executive view of program health, progress, key risks, decisions needed, and the recommended path forward.

### Contractors

Contractors review only the selected stories, criteria, tests, and discussion explicitly shared with their active registered account at a specific revision. They can comment or request changes without program access, internal sources, or full-package downloads.

### Admins

Admins govern access, user roles, audit events, disputed guidance, cost posture, model settings, and platform reliability.

## Recommended Demo Flow

### 1. Login and Quick Start

Open with the login page and explain that users now access NorthStar with assigned credentials. Access is role-based, so the experience changes depending on whether the user is an Admin, Leader, Delivery Lead, Team Member, or Client.

Then show Quick Start as the product onboarding surface.

Choose **Explore with sample data** to demonstrate **Regional Service Request Modernization** for the fictional Civic Services Network. Follow the ordinary Program Hub, Guided Plans, Studio, Leadership, Client Updates, and Client Portal screens using the sample-data banner. The scenario includes discovery evidence, delivery roles, board activity, milestones, decisions, sponsor direction, and a client publication so the relationship between inputs and outputs is visible throughout the application.

Use **Reset sample** to restore the original scenario and **Exit sample data** to return to the normal workspace. Program exploration edits remain in the current browser session and do not change live programs. Global sample mode blocks saved-package and collaboration changes. For the persisted Studio collaboration demonstration below, exit global sample mode and use a named active internal account. Access remains governed by the user's account permissions.

Key message:

> NorthStar is designed to help users understand what to do first, where to go, and how program inputs become actionable intelligence.

Show that Program Hub separates two jobs:

### 5. Studio

Show Studio as the discovery-to-product workspace. It works with or without an active program.

Highlight:

- The discovery sources shared by the fictional scenario, with extraction status and readable evidence.
- Desired output selection before analysis: roadmap, epics/features, stories/acceptance criteria, tests, gaps, and discovery analysis.
- Editable Word, Excel, and PowerPoint downloads, plus Markdown and clipboard copy.
- The synthesized capabilities, pain points, business rules, assumptions, and readiness gaps.
- Source references on epics, features, stories, and gaps, plus complete test steps and expected results.
- OpenAI evidence review and synthesis when configured, with an honest provider and quality status.
- Optional public best-practice research using a generic topic the user explicitly supplies, with citations and availability status.
- Complete saved revisions containing outputs, extracted evidence, settings, and feedback; original uploaded binary files are not retained.
- Feedback for issues, discrepancies, and changes; saved revision history and reopening a full package.
- Development review and Refinement queues, with explicit human approval and revision-specific sharing separate from AI readiness.

Suggested hands-on demonstration:

1. After **Exit sample data**, open Studio, choose **Try example evidence**, then **Save prepared example for collaboration**. Explain that this saves the labeled fictional example rather than running a new AI generation.
2. Review the full package, then download selected deliverables as an Excel workbook, Word document, or PowerPoint presentation. **Start fresh** clears the editor, not the saved package.
3. Open **Collaboration** and inspect a Development review story, its criteria, evidence, and tests. Show that an eligible AI candidate still requires the owner or Admin's explicit human approval for this exact revision; approval neither grants access nor starts development.
4. Inspect a Refinement story or request changes with a specific reason. Stage the feedback in Studio, confirm supporting evidence or a decision, and generate a new revision when ready. A comment is not an automatic source correction. New revisions reset approvals and assignments; earlier revisions remain available.
5. If a registered active reviewer account is available, share selected stories at the exact revision. No invitation email is sent; copy the review link separately. Show scoped review and Markdown export without exposing unshared work, source documents, private notes, or full Office exports. A later revision requires a new explicit share.

The preloaded output is clearly labeled as an illustrative example. Analyze the sources to demonstrate a fresh provider-generated result; successful generation by a named internal user saves a complete revision. Unsaved examples and legacy site-only work remain temporary. If OpenAI is not configured, the application identifies a provisional local draft. Failed generation preserves the existing output. Evidence review and readiness checks support human judgment; they do not certify delivery readiness. Jira handoff is not connected.

Key message:

> Studio turns discovery evidence into traceable product outputs that teams can question, refine, and take into their existing delivery tools.

## Suggested Executive Q&A

### How is this different from a dashboard?

A dashboard displays information. NorthStar interprets program signal and turns it into guidance, actions, and work products.

### How does NorthStar stay current?

Guidance refreshes when users add artifacts, save active-program updates, update the delivery board, provide leadership feedback, publish client-facing updates, or change program roles.

### How does it avoid becoming a black box?

Users can inspect inputs used, understand why guidance changed, flag disputed program guidance, and route those disputes through Admin review. In Studio, users can inspect source references, review gaps and research citations, and request corrections through feedback with revision history.

### Can users see the value before adding their own data?

Yes. Explore with sample data opens a connected fictional program in the normal application screens. Users can inspect the evidence, plans, delivery work, leadership direction, and client publication, try changes, reset the scenario, and exit to their own workspace.

### Must users create a program before using Studio?

No. Studio accepts a standalone discovery package and lets the user choose deliverables and download format. Named active internal users' successful generations save complete revisions with extracted evidence and settings. Unsaved examples and legacy site-only sessions remain temporary; original uploaded binary files are not retained.

### Does sharing a package give contractors workspace access?

No. Sharing selects specific stories at an exact revision for an existing active registered account. The recipient can review, comment, request changes, and download scoped Markdown. Internal sources, private notes, unshared stories, full Office exports, and later revisions are not included. The Contractor role requires no program assignment and grants no program access.

### Can clients use it?

Yes. Client Portal is designed as a curated executive view with program health, progress, risks, decisions, and portfolio visibility.

### Can different roles see different information?

Yes. NorthStar supports user types and program-specific role assignments. Users can have different roles across different programs.

### How is cost and reliability managed?

Admin includes cost forecasting, model settings, platform observability, audit events, and integration health indicators.
